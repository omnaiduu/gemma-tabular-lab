# Gemma results — 2026-10-16

Both Gemma 4 E2B heads finished one matched training pass. The direct price
head beat the Ridge baseline. The four-digit head did not. E4B regression
also finished, with its embedding table on CPU, and did not beat E2B. The
full write-up is `docs/blog-draft.md`.

## Protocol

- Backbone: `google/gemma-4-E2B`, revision
  `d29ff6b45f081a49ee2733a859c9c9c2d95d1a6f`.
- LoRA r=8, alpha=16, dropout=0.05, on q/k/v/o. Frozen backbone, trained
  adapter and head. FP16 compute, FP32 adapter and head.
- 1,250 optimizer steps, batch size 1, accumulation 8, seed 42, max length 256.
- Every training row was seen once. No optimizer step was skipped.
- Checkpoint chosen by validation RMSLE on 256 rows. Test is 1,000 rows and
  was not used to pick the checkpoint.
- One Tesla T4. A second T4 was visible and was not used. Peak memory 9.51 GiB.
- Torch 2.11.0+cu128, transformers 5.16.1, peft 0.20.0.
