## Findings about the digit design

The four-significant-digit codec reconstructs all 1,000 test prices exactly:
its oracle MAE and RMSLE are zero on this sample. This says nothing about
whether a trained model predicts the correct digits.

Several slots are almost constant in the training sample. A constant majority
prediction achieves 100% accuracy on both sign slots and the first exponent
digit, 97.69% on the third mantissa digit, and 99.99% on the fourth mantissa
digit. Overall slot accuracy could therefore look strong while numerical
prediction remains poor. Numerical error and exponent mistakes remain central.

Details: [pilot-analysis.json](pilot-analysis.json).
